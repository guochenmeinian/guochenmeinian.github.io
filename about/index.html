My name is Chenmeinian Guo 「郭陈美年」, a Computer Science graduate student at NYU Courant. Originally from Shanghai, I studied at the Ohio State University before moving to New York. These days, I spend most of my time working on large language models — and occasionally, on my guitar skills.

⬆ Me on Friday night

My primary interests lie in the fields of software development and LLM (Large Language Model). As a new grad, I’m excited to keep learning and find my place in this evolving field.
