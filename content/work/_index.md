## Work

Hi there 👋

Here's a short selection of my experience in software and machine learning engineering.

I now work as a machine learning engineer at TCL in Shanghai. I contributed to [TCL heyAiMe](https://www.tcl.com/global/en/tcl-design/design-works/tcl-ai-me-design-story), a modular AI companion robot.

⬆ Tezign & Meituan

I started out with software engineering. During my internship at [Bilibili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilibili), I worked on gRPC integration, audio–video decoding, and network protocols. That gave me hands-on experience with low-level systems and playback engineering.

At [Tezign](https://en.tezign.com/), I built AI agents and worked with a TypeScript stack including React, Node.js, Prisma (PostgreSQL), and Redis. It taught me to iterate quickly in production while paying attention to both functionality and product design.

At [Meituan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meituan), I worked on data preparation, model evaluation, and pre-training support for multimodal large models as part of the [LongCat-Flash-Omni](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Flash-Omni) project. The work included filtering, deduplication, importance sampling, data annealing, and pre-training experiments.
