Work

Hi there 👋

These days I work as a machine learning engineer at TCL in Shanghai. One project I've contributed to is AiMe, a modular AI companion robot.

⬆ AiMe

I began my career with my first internship at Bilibili. Here I gained exposure to the systems behind video playback, and worked on gRPC integration, audio–video decoding, and network protocols.

Building AI agents at Tezign brought me closer to the product side of engineering. I worked with TypeScript, React, Node.js, Prisma (PostgreSQL), and Redis, and learned to think about how an AI feature feels to use as well as how it works.

My last internship was with Meituan, where I contributed to their multimodal foundation model LongCat-Flash-Omni. Working on data preparation, model evaluation, and pre-training gave me hands-on experience with data filtering and deduplication, importance sampling, data annealing, and pre-training experiments.

⬆ Tezign & Meituan
